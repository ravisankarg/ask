Search is the product. The answer is a layer on top.

The user always gets the top 200 overall query matches in one smooth grid. A separate Context Picker privately distills at most 8 category-eligible records for Gemma—never replacing the results the user can browse.

01

Hard scopes stay hard

A semantic score can never bypass category, person, date, location, or MIME constraints. The device-calibrated 0.10 confidence gate stays primary; an empty positive search falls back only to nearest neighbors inside those hard scopes.

02

Precompute what is stable

Places, faces, OCR, vectors, and episodes are resolved once during preparation.

03

Bound what reaches Gemma

At most 8 records. Documents and metadata questions send text only; scenery may attach at most four 512 px images.

04

Keep internals out of prose

No G1/G2 evidence labels, planner boilerplate, filenames, or execution syntax in answers.

Query + answer flow

Inspectable before it is intelligent.

Gemma 4 E4B is the sole planner. Its category and corrected execution spec become visible before retrieval starts.

1

Classify + correct

Choose doc, scenary, person, location, or time; correct spelling, reject invented dates, and close exact-day queries with equal from/to boundaries.

2

Compile the QP spec

Emit one query_category plus the canonical C-like AST and show it below the search bar immediately.

3

Execute hybrid retrieval

For documents, every braced OCR word must occur in the same photo: {Ravi} AND {passport}. That complete conjunction is a perfect match above semantic-only fallback.

4

Publish results first

Render up to 200 overall-relevance matches without re-sorting by date: semantic-first scenery, OCR-first documents, metadata-scoped people and places.

5

Distill answer context

Join episode membership and choose no more than 8 records. Preserve full stored OCR and metadata without prompt truncation.

6

Answer naturally

Use text-only evidence for doc/person/location/time. Only scenery may attach up to four 512 px images to the clean Gemma answer conversation.

Context Picker

Maximum coverage.
Minimum repetition.

OCR indexing persists a simple media class: any image with text is doc; everything else is scenary. The QP category scopes the full result set, then episodes and relevance maximize answer coverage with minimal repetition.

Private evidence flow

One retrieval. Two deliberately different outputs.

The result browser and answer context share the corrected QP scope, then separate. The user sees up to 200 relevance-ranked matches immediately; Gemma receives a private, category-safe subset of at most eight records.

Gate

Reject before ranking

Every candidate must satisfy its category eligibility. If nothing is eligible, the evidence bundle is empty—unrelated results are never substituted.

Cover

Spend five slots on breadth

Anchor the strongest matches, then round-robin across people, places, OCR, months, and episodes before filling remaining slots by score.

Deduplicate

Prefer a new reason to answer

Stable media IDs prevent repeats. Episode membership suppresses near-duplicate moments; scenery also uses persisted SigLIP distance for visual novelty.

Decode late

Selection stays text + vectors

The picker loads no photo pixels. Only after selection may scenery decode at most four images, each reduced to a 512 px longest edge.

QP category Hard eligibility What wins inside the 8-record budget Gemma receives
doc contentClass == DOC Complete same-photo OCR conjunctions first, then text evidence, then original result rank. Full OCR + metadata; text only, no pixels.
scenary Not classified as a document Semantic relevance, persisted SigLIP visual diversity, and episode coverage. Metadata + up to four selected 512 px images.
person At least one named personLabel Strong anchors plus coverage across as many as three distinct named people. Names, time, place, OCR, and episode text; no pixels.
location A readable locationName Strong anchors plus coverage across as many as three distinct places. Place, time, people, OCR, and episode text; no pixels.
time Capture date, or modified-time fallback Strong anchors plus timeline breadth across as many as four distinct year-month buckets. Dates, place, people, OCR, and episode text; no pixels.

Episode layer: evidence construction inspects the top 100 retrieved candidates and may retain up to 32 episode representatives for context selection. Compact episode summaries are added to the final answer prompt only for event-noun plus count/list intent, capped at 12 episodes.
